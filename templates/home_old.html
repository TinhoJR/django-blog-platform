Aligned in faith

with a passion to serve

LifeSure is here to help you be your best - providing health, retirement and disability benefits that support our shared values. As your dedicated non-profit ministry partner, created by The Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod to serve you, we're in it for life: focusing on better outcomes for you - not profits.

Get connected to the best care for you.

Your well-being is our ministry

Connecting you to your benefits

To serve well, it's important to be your best self – and that's exactly what we're here for – to help care for you physically, emotionally and financially.

Serving your ministry

We bring together thousands of ministries of all sizes, enabling us to offer you affordable, quality benefits, as well as business solutions that allow you to focus on what really matters.

Thank you for your years of service

Concordia Plans cares for you in your retirement with income benefits, health benefits and resources designed specifically for you.

Our purpose is serving you

With the goal of healthy church workers serving in thriving ministries, CPS offers quality health care, financial programs and business solutions so you can concentrate on your service in His name.

Dedicated to LCMS organizations and church workers

CPS listens to your needs and cares for your total wellness so you can focus on your mission. You are our priority, and we are committed to continually exploring best-in-class solutions to help you be well to serve well.
